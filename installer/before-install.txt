Hub manages your mouse, headset and audio channels from one tray app.

DEVICES (no extra software needed)
  - Razer mice (verified: Viper V3 Pro; other Razer mice get the features they report)
  - Logitech headsets on the LIGHTSPEED "Centurion" protocol (verified: PRO X 2 LIGHTSPEED)
  Quit the vendor apps (Razer Synapse, Logitech G HUB) so they don't fight Hub over the devices.

AUDIO CHANNELS (optional)
  Game / Chat / Media channels with their own EQ, limiter and ChatMix need two free tools.
  Install them as administrator, then restart Windows:
    1. VoiceMeeter Potato   https://vb-audio.com/Voicemeeter/potato.htm
    2. Equalizer APO        https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/
       In its Configurator tick "Voicemeeter Input", "Voicemeeter AUX Input" and
       "Voicemeeter VAIO3 Input".
  Hub's Audio page walks you through the rest and shows what is still missing.
